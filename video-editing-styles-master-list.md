# The Master List — Video Editing Styles, Formats & Client Verticals

A reference for deciding **which narrow skill to build**. One skill cannot do a
podcast *and* an Apple product film — they are different crafts with different
pacing, look, and rules. This list maps the whole landscape on four axes, then
proposes the narrow skills worth building.

**How to read it:** every real job is a *combination* of axes —
`[editing style] × [format/purpose] × [client vertical] × [platform]`.
E.g. a real-estate listing reel = social-first editing × listing tour × real
estate × 9:16 Reels.

## A · The 14 editing styles (the "how it is cut")

| # | Style | Signature | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Narrative / continuity** | Cuts serve story logic; cause and effect | films, brand docs, case studies |
| 2 | **Montage** | Rhythmic sequences to music; time compression | recaps, travel, fitness, "day in the life" |
| 3 | **Jump-cut / retention** | Abrupt cuts, dead air removed, constant motion | vlogs, YouTube, Shorts, explainers |
| 4 | **Kinetic** | Rapid cuts + zooms + graphics landing like punches | hype, esports, product drops |
| 5 | **Minimalist / slow** | Long holds, stillness, few cuts | luxury, documentary, real estate flagship |
| 6 | **Documentary** | Interviews + observational footage + B-roll | brand stories, testimonials, causes |
| 7 | **Talking-head + B-roll** | A-roll spine, B-roll illustration, graphics | tutorials, thought leadership, corporate |
| 8 | **Cinematic** | Graded, scored, movie grammar | travel, product films, fashion, automotive |
| 9 | **Social-first** | Vertical, hook in 1s, captions, 1–3s cuts | Reels, Shorts, TikTok |
| 10 | **Gaming / streaming** | Picture-in-picture, highlights, overlays, memes | esports, streamers, gaming |
| 11 | **Parallel / cross-cutting** | Intercutting two threads to build tension | ads, docs, sports |
| 12 | **Hybrid** | Deliberate mixing of the above per segment | anything long-form |
| 13 | **Found-footage / mockumentary** | Handheld, "real" texture | comedy, UGC-style ads |
| 14 | **Abstract / mood** | Tone over message; art for its own sake | fashion films, music, brand teasers |

**The cuts themselves** (used inside every style): standard, jump, match,
cross-cut, J-cut (audio leads), L-cut (audio trails), hard cut, whip pan,
mask wipe, stinger.

## F · The narrow-skill map — which skills to actually build

Grouped by demand. Each is a **complete narrow skill** (its own tokens, motion
rules, pipeline, QA), not a section in a mega-skill.

### Tier 1 — highest demand (build first)
1. **Apple-style UI / product motion** — product films, feature reveals, app
   launches. Apple Standard tokens, springs, cutaway explains.
2. **SaaS / B2B demo & explainer** — product demos, onboarding, UI walkthroughs.
   Simulated screen recording, cursor physics, real-app logic, readability law.
3. **Short-form retention** (Reels / Shorts / TikTok) — hooks, jump cuts, zoom
   punches, kinetic captions, keyword highlighting, pattern interrupts.
4. **Podcast & talking-head** — multi-cam switching, jump-cut pacing, clip
   extraction, waveform/lower-thirds, captions, hybrid motion-graphics inserts.
5. **Corporate / brand film** — brand stories, culture, investor, documentary
   grammar, interview-led, B-roll, graded.
6. **E-commerce / DTC ads** — direct-response structure, UGC, product demo,
   sound-off readable, offer-led.

### Tier 2 — vertical-specific (build as clients demand)
7. **Real estate** — listing tours, agent brand, neighbourhood, CGI walkthrough,
   vertical cuts, drone.
8. **eSport / gaming hype** — roster reveals, tournament hype, highlight reels,
   kinetic ribbons, glitch, trophy finales.
9. **Education / course & training** — lessons, modules, course promos,
   chaptered, diagram-led.
10. **Healthcare / medical** — myth-vs-fact, practitioner and patient stories,
    compliance-aware, trust-led.
11. **Finance / insurance** — explainers, market updates, trust films, data-led.
12. **Fitness / wellness** — transformations, workouts, coaching, high-energy.
13. **Restaurant / food & hospitality** — menu reels, behind-the-counter,
    travel/hotel films, macro food, warm.
14. **Music / artist visuals** — music videos, lyric videos, tour visuals,
    visualizers, abstract/mood.
15. **Event / wedding films** — highlights, teasers, full features, emotional arc.
16. **Personal brand / creator** — vlogs, tutorials, opinion, day-in-the-life.
17. **Fashion / beauty** — lookbooks, routines, editorial, macro texture.

### Tier 3 — specialised (only when a client needs it)
18. **CGI / 3D architectural & product** — Blender/Three.js walkthroughs, off-plan
    pre-sales.
19. **Government / non-profit / PSA** — accessible, plain-language, captioned.
20. **Immersive** — 360/VR, interactive/branching video.

### The rule for each narrow skill
Every one contains exactly: **its own design tokens · its own motion grammar ·
its own editing rhythm · its own audio approach · its own pipeline · its own QA
and hard limits.** No style menu, no "default unless overridden" — one craft,
done properly. That is why the podcast failed inside a generalist skill: the
generalist had no single voice to give it.

---

## G · A note on what went wrong (why narrow wins)

The podcast edit failed because a generalist skill carries the *average* of
every style: pacing tuned for nothing, graphics tuned for nothing, audio tuned
for nothing. A podcast needs: multi-cam switching logic, spoken-word pacing,
clip extraction, waveform/level discipline, and — where graphics appear —
motion-graphic inserts that never fight the speech. None of that is "general
video editing". Narrow skills give each craft its own rhythm, and rhythm is
what makes an edit feel professional.

**Recommended next step:** pick one Tier-1 skill and build it to the same
depth as the Apple-hybrid skill — then repeat per client vertical as demand
appears.
